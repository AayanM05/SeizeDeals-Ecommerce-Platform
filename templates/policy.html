Privacy & Cookies Policy

Effective Date: April 2025

Privacy Policy

At SeizeDeals, your privacy is a top priority. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you interact with our platform.

Information We Collect
  • Personal Details: Name, email, phone, address.
  • Payment Info: Transaction ID, payment status.
  • Account Activity: Login credentials, order history, preferences.
  • Technical Metadata: IP, browser/device type, pages visited.
How We Use Your Information
  • To fulfill orders and manage transactions.
  • To offer personalized experiences and support.
  • To send important updates and promotional content (if opted in).
  • To improve services through analytics and user feedback.
Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share it with:

  • Trusted partners for order fulfillment and payment processing.
  • Authorities if legally required.

Data Security

Your data is protected through encryption (SSL), and passwords are securely hashed. While we implement strict security practices, no system is entirely immune to breaches.

Your Rights
  • View and update your account data.
  • Request deletion or restrict usage.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing communications.

Cookies Policy

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze performance, and deliver relevant content.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser that help websites recognize you and remember information like login status and preferences.

Types of Cookies We Use
  • Essential: Enable basic functions like cart and login.
  • Performance: Collect anonymous stats to improve usability.
  • Functional: Remember your preferences (e.g. language).
  • Marketing: Deliver personalized ads (only with consent).
Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings. Disabling some may affect functionality.

Third-party Cookies

We may use cookies from analytics and ad partners like Google or Meta. Please refer to their individual policies for details.

Policy Updates

We may revise this policy from time to time. Major updates will be communicated via email or on this page. Please check periodically.

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparency.
